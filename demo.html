Saurus: a language tutor that lives in your browser

By Eleonora Björnberg and Mahdi Yusuf · Built with Gemini and Supabase

Language apps teach words you'll never use, and nobody corrects you when you speak. Saurus brings the lesson to what you already read.

Try it

Press Try Saurus at the top, or Alt+S (Option+S on a Mac). This page becomes your lesson. Change the language and level with the buttons, hold the talk button to speak, or press any highlighted word.

What it does

  • Meets you at your level. A beginner sees a few key words swapped; an expert reads fluent text.
  • Talks with you about what you're reading, by voice or typing, and gently corrects you.
  • Remembers the words you get wrong, brings them back, and offers a quick drill.
  • Grows with you: the dinosaur ages from hatchling to elder, with a costume for each of eight languages.

How it's built

Gemini Live for real-time voice, Gemini Flash to rewrite the page at your level, and Supabase for memory, accounts and keeping our keys off your computer. On any website, install the Chrome extension from GitHub.

You're not bad at languages. You've just been learning them in the wrong place.
